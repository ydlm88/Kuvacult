When the flame goes out.

Is Kuvacult down right now?

This page is a live feed from the Kuvacult servers. If the ritual has begun, every service is answering. If it shows the server as down, we already know and are working on it.

What does “Something went wrong with the ritual” mean?

The Kuvacult app shows this screen when it can’t reach our server. Your lists, diary and ratings are safe. Tap Try Again once this page shows the ritual has begun.

How do I report an outage?

If this page says everything is up but the app still won’t connect, email kuvacult@gmail.com with your app version and what you were doing.
