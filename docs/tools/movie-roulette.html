Movie roulette

Place your bets. Spin for a film.

A random movie picker for nights when nobody wants to choose. Put a genre or two on the table, or spin your own list, and the wheel decides.

How it works

1Choose genres from our cult classics, or switch to “Your list” and paste your own films.
2Spin. Every film on the table has the same odds.
3Wherever the ball lands, that’s tonight’s movie.

Questions

How does the movie roulette work?

Pick one or more genres, or paste your own list, then spin. The wheel lands on a random pocket and gives you one film to watch tonight. Every film on the table has the same odds.

Can I spin my own list of movies?

Yes. Switch to “Your list”, paste one title per line, and spin. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Can I filter by more than one genre?

Yes. Select as many genres as you like. Any film tagged with one of them goes on the wheel.

What if we still can’t agree?

Use the blackjack movie night picker. Everyone plays a hand and the winner’s film is what you watch.
