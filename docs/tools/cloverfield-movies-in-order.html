Questions

Are the Cloverfield movies connected?

Loosely. They share a universe and themes but follow different characters, so each one works on its own.

Why does The Cloverfield Paradox come first?

Its space-station experiment is presented as what tears open reality and lets the creatures in, so it sets up the other two films.
