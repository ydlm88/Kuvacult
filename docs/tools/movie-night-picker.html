Movie night picker

Can’t agree? Gamble for it.

A free movie night picker for when your group is stuck between films. Play on one device or from separate phones with a room code. Whoever gets closest to 21 picks what you watch tonight.

    Questions

    How does the movie night picker work?

    Enter the films that are tied and who picked each one. Everyone plays a hand of blackjack against the house. The player closest to 21 without going over wins, and their film is what you watch.

    Can friends join after the game has started?

    Yes. Anyone with the room code can join at any time. If a hand is already in play, they take a seat and are dealt in on the next hand.

    What happens if two players tie?

    The tied players deal again until one of them wins.

    Is this the same as the game in Kuvacult?

    This is a demo of the blackjack tiebreaker from Veto Sacrifice in Kuvacult. In the app, your friends play their own hands from their own computers.
