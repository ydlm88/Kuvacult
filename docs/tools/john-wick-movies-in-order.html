Questions

Where does Ballerina fit in the John Wick timeline?

Ballerina takes place during and just after Chapter 3 – Parabellum, before Chapter 4.

Do I need to watch The Continental?

No. It is a prequel about the hotel in the 1970s and is not needed for the films.
