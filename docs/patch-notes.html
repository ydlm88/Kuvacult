Patch notes

What’s new in Kuvacult.

Every update, written up before you hit download. Newest at the top.

Latest
v0.9.0
Open BetaNovember 2026 · Windows
Download v0.9.0

Last stop before iOS.

This is the last big update before Kuvacult lands on iPhone. You get a roulette wheel for when nobody can pick, a rebuilt watchlist screen, a new loader, a much better Letterboxd import, and a pile of speed fixes.

One schedule change: dynamic themes are moving to January 2027, or December 2026 if things go well. See the dev note for more details.

New
Roulette wheel

Can’t pick? Spin.

I redesigned the random button from scratch. Hit Spin the wheel on any tab and the ball bounces across your posters until it lands on something. It’s live for everyone in the list, so when one person spins, every member sees the same spin and the same pick. Then make it next, spin again, or call it not tonight.

Redesign
Watchlist screen

Your watchlist, rebuilt.

The watchlist screen got new animations and smooth infinite scroll. Big lists keep loading as you scroll, so you never hit a wall halfway down. Whatever’s next still sits up top, and ordered lists keep their numbers in both grid and list view.

Redesign
New loader

Posters used to pop in one at a time. Now a little slot reel spins while they load, and the whole list shows up at once. It’s easier on your PC too.

Letterboxd import

Bring your whole history over.

The old import would miss films or mix them up with similar titles in our database. It’s now 95% accurate, and only really obscure films slip through. Your lists also fill in on screen while it runs. If anything’s missing, reach out and I’ll add it ASAP.

Performance

Faster all round.

Lots of fixes under the hood. Big watchlists should feel much snappier.

Search got better too. It now finds even the most niche of niche films, so you can be super performative and annoy your friends.

In the works
Mini games

Two new rituals in development.

I’m building two new mini games to be added to rituals, so blackjack won’t be your only way to settle an argument. They’re a lot more tactical and engaging than what’s there now, so you’ll actually be making choices that matter. They still run on chance, though, so no two matches play out the same. The first one is planned to launch with the iOS release. The second is slated for early 2027, if it doesn’t make it in with dynamic themes.

Dev noteYoshua · @Ydlm88
“I really wanted iOS to launch stable before I tear the UI apart for dynamic themes. If I rebuilt everything now, iOS would get pushed back even further, and you guys have waited long enough. If you find any major bugs, please send them to kuvacult@gmail.com and I’ll get on it. Thank you guys for all the support, it honestly means the world to me.”
