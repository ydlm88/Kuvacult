Which one is for you?

If you mostly log films on your own, from your phone or a browser, and want the biggest community of reviewers, Letterboxd does that well.

If you watch with the same group of friends, Kuvacult is the upgrade. You keep the diary, ratings and reviews, bring your Letterboxd history with you, and get the parts Letterboxd doesn’t have: one watchlist everyone can add to, a game that settles what to watch, and a room to watch it together. There is no subscription.

Switching takes seconds

1Open Letterboxd in your browser and sign in.
2Go to Settings → Data → Export your data.
3Download the ZIP file.
4Choose the ZIP on Kuvacult’s import page.

Questions

Is Kuvacult a Letterboxd upgrade?

Think of it as an upgrade. Kuvacult keeps the diary, ratings, reviews and lists you know from Letterboxd, imports your history, and adds what Letterboxd doesn’t have: watchlists friends can edit, a veto game for picking a film, and watch-together séances.

Can I move my Letterboxd data to Kuvacult?

Yes. Export your data from Letterboxd under Settings → Data, then choose the ZIP on Kuvacult’s import page. Your watched films, ratings and watchlist come across.

Can I use Letterboxd and Kuvacult together?

Yes. Many people keep their Letterboxd profile and use Kuvacult for planning and watching movies with friends.

Does Kuvacult have a subscription?

No. Every feature is free. Supporter tiers are optional one-time purchases.
