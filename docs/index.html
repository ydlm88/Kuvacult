Watchlists

Lists your friends can actually edit.

Make a list, share the code, and everyone adds to it. Changes show up live. Bring your Letterboxd history with you so you don't start over.

Reviews

Say what you actually thought.

Star ratings, rewatch tags, and a feed of what everyone else is watching. Short takes welcome.

Search

Find shows from our massive catalog.

Search films and shows, then drop them straight into any list with one tap.

Veto Sacrifice

Sacrifice what you'd rather die than watch.

Everyone picks, then everyone vetoes until one film is left. Tied? Settle it with blackjack.

Séance · Beta

Light the candle. Watch together.

Start a séance and friends join one room. Share your screen to watch media you own from your own machine. Screen share is in beta, up to 30fps, and Kuvacult never hosts or stores your files.

Profiles

Your taste, on display.

Pick four posters for your banner, show off your reviews, lists and watch history, and follow your friends.
