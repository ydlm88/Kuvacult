Social film diary & movie tracker for Windows

Kuvacult is a free Windows app for tracking movies with your friends. Rate and review every film you watch, share watchlists your friends can edit, pick what to watch with a veto game, and stream movies together in a séance, Kuvacult’s watch party room.

I
The diary

Say what you thought.

Log every movie you watch with a star rating, a rewatch tag and an optional review. Your home feed shows what your friends are watching and how they rated it.

Tap to enlarge
II
Shared watchlists

Watchlists your friends can edit.

Create a watchlist and add friends by name, or share its six-letter code with anyone. Everyone on the list can add films, and every change syncs live for the whole group.

Tap to enlarge
III
Veto Sacrifice

Sacrifice what you’d rather die than watch.

Can’t decide what to watch? Choose a watchlist, take turns vetoing films with your friends, and watch the last movie left. Ties are settled with a game of blackjack.

Tap to enlarge
Tap to enlarge
IV
Séance · Beta

Light the candle. Watch together.

A séance is Kuvacult’s watch party. Invite friends into a room and share your screen to watch movies you own together. Screen sharing is in beta at up to 30fps, and Kuvacult never hosts or stores your files.

V
Profiles

Your taste, on display.

Your profile shows your film count, reviews, watchlists and watch history, under four poster banners you choose.

Import from Letterboxd

Don't start over.
Bring your history.

Upload your Letterboxd export and Kuvacult brings over your watched films, ratings and watchlist.

1Open Letterboxd in your browser and sign in.
2Go to Settings → Data → Export your data.
3Download the ZIP file.
4Choose the ZIP on Kuvacult's import page.
A single watched.csv, watchlist.csv or ratings.csv works too.
